# Urban Planning Concepts
- Urban planning, at its simplest, its the process of designing and regulating the use of space within a city.
- It can design roads, regulate how people move, plan infrastructure, place buildings, determine building characteristics, organize public spaces and even suggest legislation
- Planning usually tackles the macro level, and leaves design details to urban design. It can create whole cities ex novo, attempt to incremental improvements, attempt to anticipate future problems and tackle individual districts or sectors of urban reality.

- Building systems determine how buildings are arranged in their plots; They also include rules regarding heights, gardens, etc.

- Building Line is the limit which a building cannot extend.

Those rules created by the urbanism of the city will affect on how those buildings are shaped:

- [Jettying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jettying) is the most medieval Europe was the result of municipalities taxing buildings depending on their face's dimension. Jettying expanded living space without costing more taxes. Jettying increased the floorspace of upper floors, making the most available land, while providing cover from the elements for people crossing bellow. It deeply influenced the image of the medieval city.

# On Architecture
Architecture is the art and science of design, and often constructing buildings

- In architecture,  rhythm refers to a repetition of elements like forms, colors, shapes and lines. Rhythm is very obvious in edifices like the Colosseum.
- Architectural style is a set of characteristics and features that make a building historically identifiable. Modernism, neoclassicism and eclecticism are all architectural styles.
- Distinct architectural styles were used to differentiate public and private buildings in ancient Athens. They did that in Dishonored's Dunwall:

Architecture deeply influences the image of the city, and showcases its history and ideology

Architecture is influenced by several factors including dominant ideologies, history, available materials, climate, political economy, space limitations and topography

- Architecture also commonly implies function, and thus makes world more legible. You look to an building and you can more or less know what the building does
- Architecture reflects and supports social life. Architecture can further enforce the separation of the public and the private sphere, or help their borders became even more porous.
- Organizations like the arabinan courtyard or common kitchen spaces in modernist Chinese architecture help foster sociability on the immediate neighborhood level.

# Topography & Natural Geography:
- Natural realities are a major factor in the shape, structure, and functions of any city
- River bends can determine building orientations, cliffs are often perfect for an acropolis, flatlands have simpler transportation needs, and a place's overall topography can make a settlement interesting or even beautiful.
- Nature and society intertwine; a natural harbor is the only important when a culture has developed sailing.
- Topographic features (rivers, cliffs, chasms, etc) have often served as edges and borders in cities both real and virtual.

# Urban Metrics for Virtual City
 - The metrics of the urban environment, not unlike the metrics of level design, in general, allow us to scale things. Metrics are important, a convention to standardize the spaces we are building.
 - From lane widths and the number of lanes on a boulevard, to city populations the of rough radius (or even area) of major city, and its building heights and densities, certain numbers do mater.
 - In-game time is an excellent metric, as is a game's overall level of abstraction. Also the degree of stylization influences metrics, and our expectations from game worlds.

# City Life
- Cities are much more than their built environment, and infrastructure. They transcend their plans, infrastructure, architecture and roads. Cities are also the humans living in and using them; the concept of the polis
- A city is everything that happens within it; Everything that shapes and is shaped by it. A city is a huge complex, dynamic stage of human life, activity and drama.
- If the humans of a city gather their things and move from a location, the city has moved location.
