# How to do I structure my Practices:

There's some categories that a musician should be focus when studying bass:
- Technique
- Fretboard Knowledge:
	- Chord Tones
	- Scales / Modes
- Time / Groove -> Metronome
- Performance -> You should always choose to learn a music that you don't know how to play
- Walking Bass Lines
- Soloing / Improvisation
- Transcription
- Genres / Styles
- Harmony / Theory

## Daily Practice Diary:
A daily practice would be best if kept in a diary format with the following entrances:
- How long?
- What key categories?
- Any performance records?
- Thoughts and observations

Its important to remember the [Pareto Principe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pareto_principle), where 20% of the time will bring 80% of the results
