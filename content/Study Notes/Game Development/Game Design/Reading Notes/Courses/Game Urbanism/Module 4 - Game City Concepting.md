The first step to concepting a city is to answer the [[11 Questions Defining a Game City]], after that is possible to start to create a reference board. To create a reference board Konstantino propose having 4 sections:
- Moodboard / Overall Aesthetics
- Architecture
- Transportation & Infrastructure
- Urbanism / Planning / Structure

I learned on the concept art course that the level of technology of a civilization can be indicated by how they construct their arches, windows, doors and columns. Its important to have a section about materials, so we can discuss how those materials and technology will mend together. This way the Architecture section inside the board will have the following subsections:
- Materials & Tecnology
- Arches
- Windows
- Doors
- Columns

After we have more or less a definition of the districts that will be present on the city we can start to do gather references for each district. 

❗ Always consider that cities are not exclusively organized on a district by district level. Important intracity & cross-district elements can deeply influence how they look and function. We should always know what tie our city together.

He also suggest that we start to think about some of the strolls that a player can have on the map, almost like a fake quest, trying to imagine how a player could traverse the map.
