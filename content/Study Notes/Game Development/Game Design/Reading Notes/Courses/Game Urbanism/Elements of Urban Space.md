This is a compiled list of finds of some of common elements one would expect to find in the contemporary and the medieval city.

**The Contemporary City**
- Roads, highways, pavements, street furniture, garages, parking spaces, bike lanes, gas stations
- Automobiles, bicycles, motorbikes, buses, trucks, trams, metro, light rail systems
- Single-family homes, apartment buildings, residences of different types for different classes, slums, informal dwellings, villas
- Factories, logistics centers, warehouses, small industry, powerplants, electrical power transmitters
- Offices, back offices, home working, farmlands
- Shops of different sizes, malls, mega-shops, supermarkets, marketplaces
- Parks, squares, open spaces, unbuilt spaces, places under construction, playgrounds, gardens
- Schools, kindergartens, universities, colleges, technical schools
- Mosques, churches, temple, cemeteries
- Bars, clubs, restaurants, cinemas, theatres, stadiums, concert halls, operas, theme parks, zoos
- Airports, ports, tunnels, canals, bridges, lighthouses
- Mobile phone towers, post offices, billboards, water towers, treatment plant, hospitals
- Police stations, prisons, fire stations, junkyards
- City hall, embassies, museums, ministries, headquarters

  

**The Medieval City**

- Cathedral, churches, monasteries, cemetery, mortuaries, shrines
- Town hall, squares, prisons
- Walls, toll gates, barracks, training grounds, city guard tower, observation towers
- Stables, bridges, farrier workshops, wagon repair workshops, roads
- Marketplaces, workshops, blacksmiths, warehouses
- Fields, granaries, gardens
- Guild houses, education institutes (the first universities were created in the medieval period)
- Houses, palaces, bathhouses, taverns, noble houses, poorhouses, orphanages
- Wells, cisterns, belltowers
