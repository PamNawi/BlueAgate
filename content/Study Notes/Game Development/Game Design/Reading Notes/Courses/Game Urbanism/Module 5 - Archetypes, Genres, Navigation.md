# City Archetypes
https://www.numberanalytics.com/blog/urban-archetypes-guide
An urban archetypes refer to repeating patterns and forms that appears on urban environments. They can refer to societal behaviors, prosperity, functions, locations, transportation modes, architecture and even neighborhoods, technologies, preconceptions on public space and specific elements. They are important because they embody the collective memory and identity of a city, shaping the way their citizens interact with the environment and each other. Urban can be seen as a manifestation of a city's history, culture and values, making them an essential aspect of urban planning and design.
