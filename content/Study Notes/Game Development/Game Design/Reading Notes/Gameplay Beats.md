_"The gameplay beats show the main events and activities that the player will undertake and how these are spaced out and timed in your level. This also covers the narrative beats; the storytelling within your level, as ideally they will be intertwined with gameplay."_  

Its important to understand what will be the rhythm imposed on a stage and how each section is spaced as the player progress. Normally the gameplay beats are presented using a line or a bar graph to understand how the narrative and the game is moving along Like the following one presented on [World of Level Design](https://www.worldofleveldesign.com/categories/wold-members-tutorials/peteellis/level-design-pacing-gameplay-beats-part1.php)
