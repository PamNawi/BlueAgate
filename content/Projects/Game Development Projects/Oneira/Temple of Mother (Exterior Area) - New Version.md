The Temple of Mother is a main area inside the [[Projects/Game Development Projects/Oneira/index|Oneira]] that I made. The original description made by the writer is very ominous and should express the very controlling behavior of the mother.

_"Tracing a path through the canyon, Edmund appears multiple times, overcome with doubt, begging the Dreamwalker to stop, as they force him to move forwards. The temple rises out of the water at the canyon's end, taking the shape of a woman with an unreadable expression."_

From the original version of the game we had a concept art of how the entrance should be looking like and when I arrived we already had the model of the temple.

![[Pasted image 20250826171808.png]]

During the rework of the Mushroom Forest that was one of the first vistas that I wanted to plan, I knew that I wanted to make a natural movement from the village for the depth where this temple should be located.

![[Pasted image 20250902164714.png]]

In a way this area reminds me a lot of [[Week 09 + 10 - Assignment]], which is a piece that I enjoyed the result and the process. Which made me want to add some waterfalls in the area and replicate the some of the concepts from there.

![[Pasted image 20250902164652.png]]
![[Pasted image 20251121171231.png]]
