# User Story
As a Player I want to collect all collectables as I explore the game world so that I can discover all the story arc and complete the trauma image
# Acceptance Criteria:
- The collectables should be visible on the map when the player camera look at them.
- The collectables should be shown on the respective UI for them
- The collectables should be saved as the player progress collecting them.
- The collectables should be easy for the art team to swap their appearance

## Anomaly Crystals:

- Not sure if they should have a small VFX, they can be occluded by the environment.
