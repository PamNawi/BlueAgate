## [Baile Zumbi!](https://globalgamejam.org/games/2026/baile-zumbi-1)
During January I participated on another edition of [Global Game Jam](https://globalgamejam.org) it was a pleasure as always to participate on the event and help with the organization. During the event I collaborate with [Malu](https://www.behance.net/malumaniglia) an old student of mine, a programmer and 2 musicians to make a game called "Baile Zumbi!". The premise is that it was a party before the [Zombie Walk](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombie_Walk) where people was already dressed as zombies and someone was turning people into actual zombies, the mission of the player was to identify who was the culprit while interacting with the people on the party. We mixed some mechanics from the game Clue, Guess Who and "Where in the World is Carmen Sandiego?" and used both physical media as the digital one. The player used the computer to run the rules and a small board so he could discuss with the other players who was culprit.

## [[Study Notes/Game Development/Game Design/Reading Notes/Courses/Level Design For Games/index|CGMA Level Design Course]]


<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/FbNbye9b4N8?si=GHLP3RM7mlquxl2L" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>

This project is a level design study developed during the final two weeks of the [[Study Notes/Game Development/Game Design/Courses/Level Design For Games/index|CGMA Level Design Course]] during 2024 , using Unreal Engine 5.3. Combining jungle and sci-fi themes suggested by the instructor, the focus was on creating visual compositions that guide the player, as there was no combat system implemented. Additionally, I aimed to incorporate subtle storytelling elements throughout the environment to enhance the immersive experience. You can see all the assignments on [[CGMA Assignments]].
